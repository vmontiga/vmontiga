# Hi, I'm Vincent 👋

**From modelling molecules to modelling data.**

I'm a junior data analyst / data engineer based near Cologne, Germany. Before data, I spent three years as a doctoral researcher in computational chemistry, simulating lanthanide molecules and analysing large result sets in Python and Fortran. Today I work on the French market at Cremer, where I also manage the product data of our webshop. In 2026 I completed Ironhack's **Data Science & Machine Learning** bootcamp.

🔎 **Open to junior data analyst and data engineering roles**, ideally in international teams (English · French · German).
